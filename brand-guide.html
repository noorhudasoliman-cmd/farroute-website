Farroute
Brand guide Version 1.0 · October 2026

This guide covers everything a designer needs to build the Farroute website, ads, Instagram posts and LinkedIn job postings so that every piece looks like one brand. The source files are in the brand folder of this project.

1. Logo

Farroute
Farroute
The markParcel Truck. A box truck whose cargo is a taped kraft parcel, with a dashed route arrow along the base. VersionsEspresso on cream (default). Cream on espresso (dark backgrounds). The window, wheel gaps and route dashes always take the background colour. LockupsHorizontal: mark left, wordmark right, gap equal to one wheel width. Stacked: mark above wordmark, centred. The mark can be used alone for app icons, favicons and profile pictures. Clear spaceKeep empty space around the logo equal to the height of one wheel on every side. Minimum sizeMark alone: 24 px tall on screen, 8 mm in print. Horizontal lockup: 28 px tall. Filesbrand/farroute-mark.svg, farroute-lockup-horizontal.svg and farroute-lockup-stacked.svg, each with a -white version (cream on espresso). Outline the wordmark text before print.
Do not Recolour the parcel or the tape. Put the logo on photos or busy backgrounds. Use pure black or pure white. Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows. Add a slogan inside the lockup.

2. Colour

Use the colours in roughly these proportions: 60 percent cream, 25 percent espresso, 10 percent kraft, 5 percent airmail blue. Blue is the attention colour. Use blue for one element per screen or post, never as a background.

4. Graphic elements

Route lineA curved kraft line, 4 to 5 px on screen, with a soft cream outline. Dashes are thin and square-ended, like road markings. Routes are drawn as smooth upward arcs, never as zigzags. ParcelThe 3D kraft box from the logo, with light top, mid front and deep side faces, and blue tape. Use it as an icon for parcel sizes and as the moving marker on maps. StampA rotated double-border rubber stamp in espresso or kraft, set in uppercase: Routes where none exist. Use it on packaging, footers and as a sign-off on social posts. MapsLight and quiet: warm water, near-white land, thin borders, no grid lines and no texture. Round cream badges with a soft shadow mark the shop and the home. CornersButtons and inputs 10 to 12 px. Cards 14 to 16 px. Chips and pills fully rounded. ShadowsOnly on floating items over maps: 0 2 6 px at 16 percent espresso. Cards on the page use a 1 px line, not a shadow. ImageryNo generic stock photos. The homepage opens with a promotional video. Otherwise, use the parcel, routes and maps as illustration.

7. Copy and voice

HeroNothing is out of reach. MissionWe create routes, you order anywhere. ActionRoute anything. StampRoutes where none exist.
Voice rules, non-negotiable No em dashes. Split into separate sentences instead. No quotation marks in copy, including error messages. Use expanded forms: write do not, not the contraction. Never use a bare pronoun. Name the noun. Use they only with a clear antecedent. Never frame stores as the villain and never name a retailer as a blocker. Write that an order is blocked. Use concrete mechanism language. Say what actually happens.
Product boundaries for every ad and post Farroute is never a tax or duty avoidance tool. Duties and taxes are set by customs in the destination country and paid by the customer. Never imply reduced, avoided, hidden or negotiated duties. Never show or promote goods that cannot travel by air: perfume, aerosols, loose batteries, food, oversized goods.
